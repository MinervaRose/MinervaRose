I work at the intersection of **applied AI, data, computational research, and technical education**.

My work focuses on systems that help people **inspect evidence, understand uncertainty, and make more informed decisions**. I am particularly interested in **auditable, interpretable, reproducible, and human-centred systems**, especially in contexts where technical depth and judgement both matter.
